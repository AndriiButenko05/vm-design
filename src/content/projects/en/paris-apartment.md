This compact Paris apartment was replanned to create a more open living space.

Large sliding doors in black glass can separate the sitting area when needed, while adding a strong graphic element to the interior.

The design draws on Art Deco through geometric lines and framed wall details. White and black stone, Italian tiles and ceramic finishes add contrast, while the light palette keeps the apartment bright and airy. Dark accents in the kitchen give the space definition without overwhelming it.

The studio developed the complete interior design project and provided design supervision throughout construction.
