Located by the port in Cannes, this Christina Beauty Space was created within older premises that needed a new layout.

The space was replanned to accommodate an office area, a beauty space and two separate treatment rooms.

White, the brand’s signature colour, became the guiding element of the interior. White walls and a seamless poured floor create a light, unified setting, while transparent shelves display the products without adding visual weight.

Maryna Vashchenko led the project from start to finish. She developed the interior design, selected the materials and supervised the renovation on site through to completion.
