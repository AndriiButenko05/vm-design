The owners of this approximately 100 m² apartment in central Nice wanted to renovate it in a style inspired by Parisian chic.

The project centres on a new kitchen and completely redesigned bathrooms, while preserving the existing marble flooring and restoring the original parquet.

Small-format tiles bring detail to the bathrooms, and a black-and-white chequerboard floor gives the kitchen its Parisian character. New doors were selected to bring the updated spaces together and create a consistent look throughout the apartment.

Maryna Vashchenko developed the design and technical drawings and provided design supervision during part of the renovation.
