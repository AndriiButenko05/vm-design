The renovation of this villa in Nice began with a complete rethink of its layout.

The staircase was reconfigured, and the kitchen and living area were expanded to create a more generous space for everyday life. Removing the existing lift opened up room for an entrance hall on the ground floor and an additional room upstairs.

Calacatta Gold and Emperador Dark marble give the interior its depth, while decorative plaster mouldings bring detail throughout the house. A column that had to remain in place was clad in mirrors, becoming a considered feature of the new design.

Maryna Vashchenko led the project from beginning to completion, overseeing the design, technical drawings, material selection and renovation on site.
