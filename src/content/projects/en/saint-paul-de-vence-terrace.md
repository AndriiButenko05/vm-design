Set in an older building, this apartment is being redesigned as a rental property.

The project calls for a complete renovation: a new layout, extensive demolition, updated technical systems and a rebuilt terrace. Each change has been planned around the practical demands of the existing building.

The view over the historic village of Saint-Paul-de-Vence shaped the new layout. The kitchen and living area have been positioned to make the most of it, giving future guests a place to gather with the village in sight.

The design and technical drawings are complete, and the project is ready for construction.
