Designed for rental use, this 58 m² apartment required a complete rethink of its layout.

Removing a long, narrow corridor freed up space for an open-plan living area, while the rooms were reorganised and the bathroom enlarged.

The new layout makes the apartment feel more connected and gives each part of the home a clearer purpose. The design also takes the existing services and technical constraints into account, preparing the apartment for renovation.
