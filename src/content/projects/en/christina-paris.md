Located on Rue Saint-Honoré in central Paris, this 100 m² beauty space was created through the renovation of an existing salon.

The design worked with what was already there: furniture was restored and repainted, becoming part of the renewed interior.

One section was reconstructed and replanned as an open space for training cosmetologists. The project brought a new function to the premises while making thoughtful use of its existing elements.

Maryna Vashchenko led the project in full, from planning and interior design to technical drawings, furnishing and supervision of the renovation. She stayed in Paris throughout the work to oversee its implementation on site.
