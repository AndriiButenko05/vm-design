An older space in central Rome became a private beauty space for Christina.

Its high vaulted ceilings and arches set the character of the interior, while a predominantly white palette keeps it bright and open.

A curved curtain creates a separate treatment room whenever privacy is needed. Natural marble brings texture to the light interior; its corners were cut at a precise 30-degree angle, a detail that required particular care in execution. Illuminated mirrors and sculptural lighting complete the space.

Maryna Vashchenko led the project in full, from planning and interior design to technical drawings, selection of materials and furnishings, and design supervision throughout the renovation.
