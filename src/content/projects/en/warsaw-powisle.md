The owners of this two-level penthouse in Warsaw wanted a contemporary interior that felt light, bright and free of unnecessary detail, yet warm and welcoming.

Parquet flooring, natural wood and wood veneer bring texture and warmth to the restrained design.

A modest change to the original layout gave each floor a distinct role. The lower level is open and designed for shared time, while the upper level is more private, with bedrooms and dressing rooms planned around the owners’ daily lives.
