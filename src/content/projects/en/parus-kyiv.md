A 213-square-metre apartment in the Parus residential complex on the Obolon embankment, with floor-to-ceiling windows onto the Dnipro and the Pivnichnyi bridge.

The layout was redrawn from the bare shell into an open kitchen and living room, two bedroom suites with their own dressing rooms, two bathrooms and a laundry. The palette is warm and dark: burl walnut and embossed leather in the living room, Sahara Noir in the hall, Arabescato Orobico in the master bathroom, with brass kept to thin lines and hardware.

The full design set runs to twenty-three sheets — survey, partitions, floors, ceilings, lighting, electrical and every elevation — alongside the visualisations shown here.
