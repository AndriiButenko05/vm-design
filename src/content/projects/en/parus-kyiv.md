The starting point for this spacious family apartment on Kyiv’s waterfront was a contemporary black kitchen, ordered by the owners before the interior design project began.

The challenge was to create a cohesive home around it. A small adjustment to the layout helped make the apartment more comfortable for family life.

Large-format ceramic surfaces and textile wall details add texture throughout the interior. In the living areas and corridor, custom cabinetry combines wood veneer, glossy three-dimensional panels and brass details, giving these spaces their own character while keeping the overall design connected.

Maryna Vashchenko developed the interior design and technical drawings and assisted with the selection of some furnishings and materials. The renovation was carried out according to her project.
