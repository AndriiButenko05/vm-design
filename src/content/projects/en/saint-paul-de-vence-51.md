This compact apartment in Saint-Paul-de-Vence was redesigned for rental use.

By relocating the entrance, the new layout makes room for a separate bedroom alongside an open-plan living area.

The apartment was completely replanned with relatively little demolition. The result is a clear division between living and sleeping spaces, making the most of its 51 m² while providing everything needed for a comfortable stay.

The design project and complete set of technical drawings are ready for renovation work to begin.
