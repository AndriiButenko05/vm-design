The renovation gave this apartment an entirely new character.

The kitchen and bathroom were redesigned with careful attention to how each space would be used, while new doors, windows and finishes refreshed the home throughout. The original parquet was the one element retained and restored.

The bathroom became the starting point for the interior’s visual direction. A richly coloured tile collection by Italian manufacturer Ceramica Sant’Agostino brought character to the room; its colours and pattern were then carried into the kitchen. Furnishings and fittings from Italian manufacturers complete the apartment.

Maryna Vashchenko led the design, prepared the technical drawings, selected the materials and supervised the work on site.
